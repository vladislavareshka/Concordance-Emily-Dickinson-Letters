<h2>----- 📜 About the project -----</h2>
I am glad to share an interactive, digital concordance of the surviving letters of Emily Dickinson, designed to support scholarly research and textual exploration of her work. The concordance enables users to browse, filter, and sort thousands of keyword-in-context entries extracted from Dickinson's correspondence, providing rapid access to vocabulary, expression, and usage across her epistolary writings. 
<br><br>This concordance is based on and serves as a companion to <a href="https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674982970">"The Letters of Emily Dickinson" (edited by Cristanne Miller and Domhnall Mitchell, The Belknap Press of Harvard University Press, 2024)</a>, and is intended for a wide range of users, including literary scholars, students, digital humanists, historians of American literature, and anyone interested in Dickinson's language.
<br><br>Each concordance entry presents a target word together with its surrounding context and the corresponding letter number, recipient, and date information. This should allow researchers to move beyond simple keyword searching and examine how individual words function within Dickinson's broader linguistic and stylistic practices. The interface supports filtering by letter number, addressee, date, word form, and surrounding context, making it possible to locate specific usages more efficiently.
<br><br>A dedicated "Lexeme Index" provides an alphabetical inventory of words found in the corpus, along with references to the letters in which they occur.

<h2>----- 🌱 Work in progress -----</h2>
This is an ongoing project. The corpus will be refined: features, search capabilities, interface elements, underlying data, &c. &c. may continue to evolve and change. Future development may include improved analytical tools, additional metadata, and - admittedly - further corrections to the underlying texts. Users are therefore encouraged to regard the current release as a developing research resource rather than a finalised edition.
